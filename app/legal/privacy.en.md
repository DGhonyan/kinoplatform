# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 9 October 2026

Kinoplatform is a platform where film professionals and talents create profiles, find each other, and book filming locations. It is owned and operated by Sole Proprietor (IE) Meri Karen Danielyan, Republic of Armenia ("we", "us").

This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. For any question or request about your data, write to **privacy@kinoplatform.com**.

## What we collect

**Your account**

- Email address
- Password — stored only as a one-way hash, so we can never see it

**Your profile**

- First and last name, gender, date of birth, and the city you are based in
- Profession, experience level, education, languages, and bio
- Work experience, projects, tools, equipment, and a portfolio link or file
- Profile photo and additional photos
- Practical details: whether you are willing to travel or join long shoots, and whether you have a passport, visa, or driving license
- Your availability calendar

**Appearance details (actors and other talents only)**

- Required: height, weight, and hair, eye, and skin color
- Optional: tattoos and where they are, clothing and shoe size, and other physical characteristics you choose to share

**What you do on Kinoplatform**

- Recommendations you write for other members
- Connections with other members
- Announcements you post
- Location booking requests: dates, crew size, your message, and the quoted price

**Security data**

- For each signed-in session: IP address, browser and device type, and when the session started
- When you last signed in, and the number of failed sign-in attempts

We do **not** collect your phone number or home address, and we do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.

## How we use it

- To create and run your account and show your profile
- To let other people find you in the crew and talent directories
- To handle location booking requests
- To send you service emails: verification codes, password resets, and security alerts such as a sign-in from a new device
- To keep Kinoplatform secure and prevent abuse
- To meet our legal obligations

We send news and promotional emails **only if you opt in**. You can change this at any time in Settings.

## What other people can see

Kinoplatform is a professional directory, so most of your profile is public. Anyone can see it, including visitors without an account:

- Name, photos, profession, experience level, city, education, languages, and bio
- Work experience, projects, tools, equipment, portfolio, and practical details
- Recommendations other members wrote about you
- Your availability calendar

**Never shown on your profile:** your email address, date of birth, gender, and appearance details.

**Talent search.** Visitors can filter talents by gender, age, height, weight, and hair, eye, and skin color. These details are never displayed, but if yours match a filter, your profile appears in the results, so someone could infer them. Physical characteristics are never used in search.

Recommendations you write appear on the other member's profile under your name. Announcements appear on the board after a moderator approves them. Booking requests are visible only to you and the team managing the location.

## Sensitive information

Some appearance details, especially skin color and any physical characteristics you choose to share, can reveal sensitive information such as ethnic origin or health. We use them only for casting searches, never display them to others, and never use them for advertising. Physical characteristics are optional, and you can remove them from your profile at any time.

## Who processes your data for us

We do not sell your data. We use these service providers to run Kinoplatform, and they process data only on our behalf:

- **Google Cloud** — runs our servers
- **MongoDB Atlas** — stores account and profile data
- **Microsoft Azure** — stores uploaded photos and files
- **Resend** — delivers our emails
- **GitHub Pages** — hosts the website
- **CARTO and OpenStreetMap** — show maps on location pages; your browser loads the map images directly from them, which shares your IP address with them

These providers may store or process data outside Armenia, for example in the European Union or the United States.

We may also disclose data to state authorities when the law of the Republic of Armenia requires it.

## Cookies and local storage

We use only the cookies that are strictly necessary for signing in:

- **access_token** and **refresh_token** — keep you signed in
- **mfa_trust** — remembers a device you trusted for two-factor authentication, for up to 14 days

We also keep a few items in your browser's local storage: a copy of your profile so pages load faster, your language choice, and the locations you saved. We use no advertising, analytics, or tracking cookies. If you block cookies, you can still browse public pages, but you will not be able to sign in.

## How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. Sign-in sessions expire automatically, and expired session records are cleared.

When you delete your account, we immediately and permanently delete your profile, photos and files, availability, announcements, booking requests, recommendations, and connections. Copies held by our service providers, such as email delivery logs, are deleted on their own schedules.

## Your rights

- **See and download your data:** Settings → Your data → Export
- **Correct it:** edit your profile at any time
- **Delete it:** Settings → Delete account
- **Stop promotional emails:** Settings
- **Anything else**, such as restricting how we use your data or withdrawing your consent: write to privacy@kinoplatform.com

You can also file a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Agency of the Republic of Armenia.

## Age

You must be at least 16 years old to create an account. If you are under 18, please make sure a parent or legal guardian agrees to your use of Kinoplatform.

## Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data: passwords are hashed, connections are encrypted, two-factor authentication is available, and we alert you to sign-ins from new devices. No system is completely secure. If a breach affects your data, we will inform you and the authorities as the law requires.

## Changes to this policy

If we make meaningful changes, we will update the effective date above and ask you to review and accept the new version the next time you use Kinoplatform.
